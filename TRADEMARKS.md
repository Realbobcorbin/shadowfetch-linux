Shadowfetch Linux is free software (see [`LICENSE`](LICENSE), GPL-3.0-or-later), but the
**names, logos, and distinctive visual identity are not covered by that license.**

## Reserved marks and assets

The following are reserved and are **not** licensed for reuse under the GPL:

- The names **"Shadowfetch"** and **"Umbra"**, and the "Fire Edition" branding.
- The Shadowfetch and Umbra logos, emblems, and icon marks.
- The distinctive wallpapers and artwork under `branding/`, `artwork/`, and the
  branding/theme package payloads (e.g. `packages/shadowfetch-branding`,

All rights to these names and artwork are reserved by the project maintainers.

## What you may do

You may build on, modify, and redistribute the **code and packaging** in this repository
under the terms of the GPL. This is how community forks are meant to happen.

**If you distribute a modified version, please re-brand it.** Remove or replace the
Shadowfetch and Umbra names, logos, wallpapers, and identity so that your build is clearly
your own and is not presented as an official Shadowfetch release. This is the same courtesy
that Debian, Fedora, and other distributions ask of their downstreams, and it protects users
from confusing an unofficial build with a signed official one.

## Aggregate licensing note

A Shadowfetch Linux ISO is an **aggregate** of many independent works. The GPL in this
repository covers the project's own code and packaging. Every bundled upstream Debian package
retains its own upstream license; per-package copyright and licensing is recorded in each
package's `debian/copyright`. Population of the ISO from those packages does not relicense them.
